Context

Process

Design Inspiration

I grew up in Karachi, Pakistan and my most and least favourite thing to do when I go home, is driving. In Karachi, your vehicle is the perpetual thread through the city's needle, and more than the law you abide by the rules of the road.

Rule Number One: Honk.
Honk at the right moment and even the sea will part for you. I wanted to recreate the chaos, silliness and thrill of commuting in my hometown in this game. And I combined it with the urban legend of the specter bride, who having lost her groom on her wedding night, now forever and relentlessly haunts the main road through the neighbourhood of Karsaz.

  • Goldspike Feature: Honk Mechanic
    The player can honk at nearby vehicles to make them move out of the way:
    • Moving vehicles move left or right
    • Parked vehicles move forwards
    • Wrong-way vehicles slow down
    Honking only works if you are close enough for other drivers to 'hear' it. This was intended to challenge the driver to control their speed and still run fast enough. Collisions will slow you down but if you're too cautious and safe you'll never get through.
  • The Bride
    Once encountered, the bride pursues you relentlessly and contact with her is deadly. You can't see the bride behind you, but her glow warns you when she's getting too close. Not being able to see her was intended to feed the uncertainty and anxiety of the player.
  • Player Guidance
    My approach was simply to start the game with text guidance for the controls and the story. I chose this because both the controls and the story were very simple and required very little text to communicate effectively, so there wasn't much of a risk of overwhelming or confusing the player.

Iteration from Gold Spike to Gold

  • Bike Controls: Polished controls by programming the bike to re-orient itself after a collision.
  • Bride Pathfinding: Rebuilt the bride with Unity's NavMesh for AI pathfinding, enabling her to pursue the player through traffic.
  • Mise en Scène/ Juice: Added lighting, sound and story to build mood, tension and immersion.

Reflection

  • Bride Speed: I would make her faster or spawn close to the player several times. In the current version she can be slow, and can fall far enough behind to defeat the urgency of the game.
  • Collision Damage: I wasn't able to complete collision damage, so the bike can get away with colliding into traffic. A complete damage mechanism and UI would force the player to balance dodging traffic with maintaining enough speed to outpace the bride.
