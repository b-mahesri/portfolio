Context/Assignment

Project 2: Rapid Prototype was the first original game assignment in EECS 494: Game Development and Design. The task was to individually design and make substantial progress on an original game, by delivering it's most technically difficult and novel aspect, working iteratively through two deliverables in two weeks: a Golspike and a Gold.

  • Week 2: Gold - Iterate rapidly on peer and instructor feedback, explore the design further and deliver the final prototype.
